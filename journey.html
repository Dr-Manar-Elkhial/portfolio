Career

Professional journey

From archaeology, to conservation practice, to the science that supports it.

2006

B.A. in Archaeology, First-Class Honours

Cairo University, Faculty of Archaeology

Grounding in archaeological materials and the cultural context that later shaped a conservation-first career.

2007 – 2012

Organic Material Conservator

National Library and Archives of Egypt, then Grand Egyptian Museum

Treated and documented 200+ manuscripts across Coptic, Jewish and Islamic collections; moved into organic-materials conservation at GEM, applying FTIR, multispectral imaging and microscopy to condition diagnosis.

2012 – 2023

Head, Preventive Conservation Unit

Grand Egyptian Museum

Led preventive conservation, environmental monitoring and IPM/anoxic treatment programmes for one of the world's largest museum collections; engineered Egypt's first modified-atmosphere fumigation system; led a multidisciplinary team of 10 conservators and scientists.

2019 – 2022

M.A. & Ph.D. in Heritage Science

Cairo University, Faculty of Archaeology

Doctoral research optimised modified inert-atmosphere fumigation efficacy for insect disinfestation, deepening the scientific and analytical dimension of hands-on conservation practice.

2023 – Present

Assistant Professor of Heritage Science

Ain Shams University, Faculty of Archaeology

Teach and coordinate courses on preventive conservation, environmental risk and archaeometric materials characterisation; supervise postgraduate research; mentor 20+ students.

2023 – Present

Director, International Collaboration Office

Ain Shams University, Faculty of Archaeology

Coordinate partnerships across Europe, North America and the Middle East, including the Ta'ziz project with the University of Marburg and the El-Hiba Coffins project with DAI Cairo and Fayoum University.
