Research & analytical work

My research translates laboratory analysis into practical conservation decisions — from oxygen-barrier testing for modified-atmosphere systems to non-dispersive infrared detection of active insect infestation. Selected instrumentation and methods:

pXRFFTIR (ATR/transmission)SEM-EDX Multispectral imagingND-IR spectroscopy X-ray radiographyColourimetryMicroscopy

This work has coordinated analytical assessment for 70+ complex organic objects at the Grand Egyptian Museum, and underpins ongoing collaborations including the Ta'ziz Science Cooperation Project (Ain Shams University, University of Marburg, AUB) and the El-Hiba Coffins Conservation Project (Fayoum University & DAI Cairo).
